# Sales Roleplay Test Scripts

**Instructions:** These scripts are designed to test each AI persona in the system. Copy and paste messages **one at a time**, wait for the AI customer's response, and adapt your approach based on their reactions — just like a real sales call.

**How Scoring Works:** The system evaluates you across **5 categories** (each worth up to 20 points, max 100):
1. **Rapport Building** — Greetings, empathy, active listening, personalization
2. **Needs Discovery** — Open-ended questions, follow-ups, uncovering pain points
3. **Product Presentation** — Clear value propositions, ROI, benefits, features
4. **Objection Handling** — Acknowledging concerns, reframing, providing evidence
5. **Closing** — Proposing next steps, setting timelines, asking for commitment

**Scoring Tips:**
- Use **open-ended questions** (What/How/Why) — they score higher than yes/no questions
- Use **follow-up questions** like "You mentioned X — can you tell me more about that?" for bonus points
- Follow the **sales flow order**: Opening → Discovery → Presentation → Objection → Closing
- Use **specific keywords** the system recognizes (see keyword guide at the bottom)
- Aim for **8-12 messages** per conversation for optimal evaluation

## 🟢 Persona 1: The Friendly Prospect (BEGINNER)
**Difficulty:** Beginner | **Tone:** Casual | **Trust:** High | **Patience:** High
**Traits:** Open-minded, pleasant, genuinely interested but may lack urgency. Easy to talk to.
**Likely Objections:** "Not sure if now is the right time" • "Need to discuss with my team" • "What would you recommend?" • "Which plan is best for me?"

### Phase 1: Opening / Greeting
```
Hey there! Thanks so much for taking the time to chat with me today. I really appreciate it — how's your day going so far?
```

### Phase 2: Discovery / Needs Assessment
```
That's great to hear! So tell me a bit about your situation — how are you currently handling customer relationship management? Walk me through your current process.
```

```
Interesting — and how long have you been using that approach? What challenges have you been running into with it?
```

```
You mentioned some pain points there. Can you help me understand which of those is causing the biggest headache for your team right now?
```

### Phase 3: Value Presentation
```
I really appreciate you sharing all of that. Based on what you've described, I think our CRM solution could be a great fit. It centralizes all your customer data in one place, automates follow-ups so nothing falls through the cracks, and gives your team real-time reporting and analytics. Most of our clients see about a 30% improvement in customer retention within the first 6 months.
```

```
And one of the key benefits is the time savings — our clients typically save 15-20 hours per week per team member by eliminating manual data entry and consolidating their tools. That ROI adds up fast.
```

### Phase 4: Objection Handling
```
I completely understand wanting to discuss this with your team — that's a smart move. What specific aspects do you think they'd want to know more about? I can help you put together the key points to share with them.
```

```
And regarding timing — I hear you. What I've found is that the best time to implement a CRM is before things get too painful. We actually offer full training and a phased rollout so it doesn't disrupt your current operations. Would that help ease the concern about timing?
```

### Phase 5: Closing / Next Steps
```
I'm glad this is resonating with you! Here's what I'd suggest as a next step: how about we set up a quick 20-minute demo for you and your team next week? That way everyone can see it in action and ask questions. Would Tuesday or Thursday work better for you?
```

## 💰 Persona 2: The Budget Hunter (BEGINNER)
**Difficulty:** Beginner | **Tone:** Casual | **Price Sensitivity:** Very High | **Decision Speed:** Slow
**Traits:** Constantly compares costs, seeks discounts, challenges high pricing. Needs strong ROI justification.
**Likely Objections:** "That's too expensive" • "Competitor offers cheaper" • "Can I get a discount?" • "Is it worth the cost?"

### Phase 1: Opening / Greeting
```
Hi! Thanks for making time for this call — I know your schedule is busy and I really appreciate it. How's everything going on your end?
```

### Phase 2: Discovery / Needs Assessment
```
So before we dive in, I'd love to understand your current setup. Can you tell me about what tools you're currently using for customer management? And what's working well vs. what's frustrating?
```

```
That's really helpful context. What would you say are the biggest costs — whether in time or money — that your current process is creating for your business?
```

```
You mentioned some manual processes there. How many hours per week would you estimate your team spends on those tasks? Walk me through a typical workflow.
```

### Phase 3: Value Presentation
```
That's exactly the kind of inefficiency our solution addresses. Our platform automates those manual tasks and centralizes everything, so your team gets back those lost hours. For a team of 5, clients typically save 15-20 hours per person per week — that's essentially like getting 2 extra employees for free. The ROI is usually 5-6x within the first year.
```

```
In terms of investment, we have flexible pricing tiers. Our Starter plan is $99/month with all the core features. But here's the thing — if your team is losing even 10 hours a week to manual work, that's already $400-500 in lost productivity monthly. So the platform essentially pays for itself and then some.
```

### Phase 4: Objection Handling
```
I totally understand being careful with budget — that's a smart approach. Let me address the cost concern directly: rather than thinking of it as an expense, consider the savings. You mentioned your team spends X hours on manual tasks. At average wages, that costs significantly more than the subscription. Would it help if I walked you through a specific ROI calculation for your team size?
```

```
And I hear you about wanting to compare options. What we offer that competitors don't is a 30-day free trial with full features — no credit card required. You can test it with your actual workflows, measure the real time savings, and decide based on data, not promises. If it doesn't deliver value, you walk away with no strings attached. Fair enough?
```

### Phase 5: Closing / Next Steps
```
Great question! We also have the Starter plan at $99/month if you want to begin smaller and scale up as you see results. What I'd recommend is setting up that free trial so you can see the actual numbers in your business. Shall we get that started this week? I can have you up and running in about 15 minutes.
```

## ⚡ Persona 3: The Busy Executive (INTERMEDIATE)
**Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Tone:** Formal | **Patience:** Very Low | **Decision Speed:** Fast
**Traits:** Time-constrained, wants quick concise answers, values efficiency. Will cut you off if you ramble.
**Likely Objections:** "No time for a lengthy demo" • "Summarize key benefits in one sentence" • "How quickly can we implement?" • "Cut to the chase"

### Phase 1: Opening / Greeting
```
Good morning. I know your time is valuable, so I'll keep this concise and focused. Thank you for fitting me in today.
```

### Phase 2: Discovery / Needs Assessment
```
To make sure I'm not wasting your time — I understand you're looking to improve customer retention and sales team efficiency. Is that accurate? And what's your timeline for making a decision on this?
```

```
Got it. What would you say is the single biggest bottleneck your sales team faces right now when it comes to managing customer relationships?
```

### Phase 3: Value Presentation
```
Here's the bottom line: our CRM platform can help you increase customer retention by 25-30% and cut your sales cycle time significantly. We work with over 50 companies in your industry, and the typical ROI is 5-6x within the first year. Implementation takes 2-3 weeks, not months. Would you like me to unpack the specifics, or would an executive summary be more useful?
```

```
The three key benefits that matter most for executives in your position: first, real-time reporting and analytics so you always have visibility into pipeline health. Second, automated follow-ups that ensure no deal falls through the cracks. Third, seamless integration with your existing tools — no rip-and-replace required. All of that translates to concrete savings of 15-20 hours per team member per week.
```

### Phase 4: Objection Handling
```
Completely understand you don't have time for a lengthy demo. How about this: I'll send you a 2-minute video walkthrough and a one-page ROI breakdown tailored to your team size. If the numbers make sense, we can pencil in a focused 15-minute call with you and any key stakeholders. No commitment until you've reviewed the data. Does that approach work for you?
```

```
And regarding implementation speed — we do a phased rollout specifically designed to minimize disruption. Your team can be up and running on core features within the first week, with full deployment in 2-3 weeks. We handle the migration and training so it doesn't eat into your team's productive hours.
```

### Phase 5: Closing / Next Steps
```
Here's my recommendation: let me send you that executive summary and ROI analysis today. If it looks promising, we schedule a 15-minute call next week with whoever else needs to be involved. One efficient meeting and you'll have everything you need to make a decision. When would be best — early or late next week?
```

## 🔍 Persona 4: The Detail Seeker (INTERMEDIATE)
**Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Tone:** Formal | **Patience:** High | **Decision Speed:** Slow
**Traits:** Asks many technical and specific questions. Wants to understand every feature, integration, and specification before committing.
**Likely Objections:** "How does this integrate with our systems?" • "What are the technical specs?" • "Explain feature X in detail" • "What about edge case Y?"

### Phase 1: Opening / Greeting
```
Good afternoon! Thank you for scheduling this time. I'm looking forward to having a thorough conversation about your needs. How are things going on your end?
```

### Phase 2: Discovery / Needs Assessment
```
Before I share anything about our solution, I'd like to understand your environment first. Can you walk me through your current tech stack? What systems are you using for CRM, communications, and reporting right now?
```

```
That's very helpful context. And how do those systems integrate with each other today? Are there any gaps or manual handoffs between them?
```

```
You mentioned some integration challenges. Tell me more about that — what specific data flows or processes break down when those systems don't talk to each other properly?
```

### Phase 3: Value Presentation
```
Based on what you've described, our platform is designed specifically to solve those integration pain points. We have a REST API and webhook support, plus pre-built connectors for over 40 platforms including the ones you mentioned. All your customer data flows into one centralized system with real-time syncing — no manual handoffs, no data silos.
```

```
On the technical side, we support both cloud and hybrid deployment options. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit with AES-256, we're SOC 2 Type II certified, and we offer granular role-based access controls. For reporting, we have customizable dashboards with real-time analytics, and you can also push data to your existing BI tools via our API. Would you like me to go deeper into any of those areas?
```

### Phase 4: Objection Handling
```
That's a great question about edge cases. Let me be specific: our API handles up to 10,000 requests per minute with 99.9% uptime SLA. For the integration scenario you described, we actually have a dedicated data mapping layer that handles schema differences between systems. I can share our technical documentation and architecture diagrams — would that be helpful for your team to review?
```

```
And regarding the migration concern — we do a phased rollout where we run our system in parallel with your existing setup for 2-3 weeks. That way your team can validate that all data flows correctly before we cut over. Our average customer satisfaction score for implementation is 4.7 out of 5. I can also connect you with a technical reference from a similar company in your industry if you'd like to hear their experience directly.
```

### Phase 5: Closing / Next Steps
```
I think the best next step would be a technical deep-dive session where we walk through your exact use cases and integration requirements with our solutions architect. We can do a hands-on demo using sample data that matches your scenarios. How does scheduling a 45-minute session next week sound? And are there other stakeholders from your technical team who should join?
```

## 🔴 Persona 5: The Skeptic (ADVANCED)
**Difficulty:** Advanced | **Tone:** Formal | **Trust:** Very Low | **Patience:** Low | **Decision Speed:** Very Slow
**Traits:** Highly analytical, distrusts sales pitches, demands concrete proof and third-party validation. Will challenge every claim.
**Likely Objections:** "How do I know this actually works?" • "Show me case studies" • "I've heard mixed reviews" • "What's the catch?"

### Phase 1: Opening / Greeting
```
Good morning. I appreciate you giving me a few minutes today. I know you've likely heard a lot of sales pitches, so I want to be upfront — I'm here to understand your needs first and be honest about whether we're actually a good fit. Fair enough?
```

### Phase 2: Discovery / Needs Assessment
```
Before I make any claims about our product, I'd like to understand your situation. What's been your experience with CRM solutions in the past? Have you tried any that didn't live up to the promises?
```

```
I appreciate you sharing that — those are valid frustrations. If I could understand your specific requirements better, I can tell you straight whether we can deliver or not. What are the top 2-3 things you need a CRM to actually do well, based on your real-world experience?
```

```
You mentioned [refer to what they said]. Can you help me understand what specifically went wrong with your previous vendor? What would "actually works" look like for your team?
```

### Phase 3: Value Presentation
```
I want to be transparent with you rather than just making claims. Here's what I can back up with data: we have 500+ active clients, with an average retention rate of 94% — meaning once companies try us, they stay. Specifically in your industry, I can share 3 case studies where companies saw measurable results within the first quarter. I'd rather show you evidence than just tell you we're great. Would those case studies be useful?
```

```
And rather than me telling you the ROI numbers, let me give you the tools to calculate it yourself. We publish our methodology for those 30% retention improvements and 15-20 hours saved claims — it's all based on anonymized client data that's independently verified. I can send you the full report so you can scrutinize the methodology yourself. Does that approach feel more credible than me just quoting statistics?
```

### Phase 4: Objection Handling
```
That's a completely fair concern, and I'm glad you're raising it. You asked about security and reliability — here are the facts: SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, 99.9% uptime SLA with financial penalties if we miss it. I'll send you our full security documentation and audit reports. For references, I can connect you directly with 2-3 current clients in your industry — no cherry-picked testimonials, real conversations. Would that help address the trust question?
```

```
And regarding the "what's the catch" question — I'll level with you. The catch is implementation effort. It takes 2-3 weeks and requires your team's involvement in configuration. We don't pretend it's zero-effort. What we do offer is a 60-day money-back guarantee. If you're not seeing results by day 60, you get every penny back, no questions asked. I'd rather earn your trust through accountability than through a sales pitch.
```

### Phase 5: Closing / Next Steps
```
Here's what I'd suggest, and I want you to feel zero pressure: let me set up a technical deep-dive where you can ask our product team the hardest questions you've got. I'll also send the case studies, security docs, and the methodology report upfront so you can review them before the meeting. And I'll arrange those reference calls with existing clients. If after all of that you're still not convinced, no hard feelings — we part ways. Would that be a fair process to evaluate us?
```

## 📊 Testing Checklist

For each persona test, verify:

### Scoring Categories
- [ ] **Rapport Building** (0-20 pts) — Did greetings feel natural? Used empathy and personalization?
- [ ] **Needs Discovery** (0-20 pts) — Asked open-ended questions? Used follow-ups? Uncovered real pain points?
- [ ] **Product Presentation** (0-20 pts) — Presented clear value, ROI, and benefits tied to their needs?
- [ ] **Objection Handling** (0-20 pts) — Acknowledged concerns? Provided evidence? Reframed objections?
- [ ] **Closing** (0-20 pts) — Proposed specific next step with timeline? Asked for commitment?

### Sales Flow
- [ ] Followed the 5-phase sequence (Opening → Discovery → Presentation → Objection → Closing)
- [ ] Adapted pace to persona difficulty level
- [ ] Conversation length was 8-12 messages

### AI Persona Behavior
- [ ] AI stayed in character (tone, patience, objections matched persona config)
- [ ] AI raised its configured objections during the objection phase
- [ ] AI difficulty felt appropriate (beginner = forgiving, advanced = challenging)
