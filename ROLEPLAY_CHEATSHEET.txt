------------------------------ THE 3 ENGINES ---------------------------------

A  DeBERTa NLI         Zero-shot intent / objection / active listening   ~25ms
B  Emotion-RoBERTa+GE  Empathy + emotion + pressure level                ~15ms
C  Llama 3.1 8B Q8_0   Reasoning, persona reply, coaching                ~3-5s


------------------------------ THE 9 AGENTS ----------------------------------

PER-MESSAGE (every turn):
  1. Guardrail        rules    block bad input
  2. EQ               A+B      0-100 EQ score (empathy/listening/pressure)
  3. Knowledge        RAG      fact-check trainee claims vs org docs
  4. Objection Inject rules    decide if AI raises an objection this turn
  5. Adaptive Diff    rules    warm/cool persona based on EQ trajectory
  6. Persona          C        the AI customer's reply (1 LLM call)
  7. Analyst          C/skipN  stage detection + coaching hints

ASYNC (subprocess, non-blocking):
  8. SalesRLAgent     PPO RL   live deal probability

POST-SESSION:
  9a. Performance     C        category scores + summary + coaching tip
  9b. Replay          C        annotated transcript + alternative responses
  9c. SalesRL Full    PPO RL   turn-by-turn conversion trajectory

------------------------ THE LSTM RISK MODEL --------------------------------

Architecture:  LSTM(in=27, h=64, layers=2, dropout=0.3) -> Linear(64->1)
Input:         27-dim vector per turn
               6 state + 3 willingness + 7 objection + 8 emotion +
               1 position + 1 resolved + 1 speaker
Training:      1,000 SaaS conversations (DeepMost), ~12K partial sequences
               via partial-sequence training trick (turns 1..N at each step)
               Position-weighted loss (later turns matter more)
               30 epochs, Adam, max seq=30, pack_padded_sequence
Results:       Overall acc 58.3%
               AUC-ROC 0.65
               LATE-TURN ACC 70.6%   <- THE NUMBER THAT MATTERS
               <5ms inference on CPU
Trend:         compares risk_now vs risk 2 turns ago, threshold 0.08

WHY: per-turn classifiers miss DRIFT. Engagement can be high right now but
falling over time. The LSTM catches trajectory; classifiers catch snapshots.

------------------------ HYBRID 3-TIER EVALUATION ---------------------------

Tier 1: NLP rules    (questions, keywords, flow)
Tier 2: ML/NLP       (sentiment, NER, dialogue acts)  -> merged into T1
Tier 3: LLM          (Performance Agent + Replay Agent)

  FINAL = 60% NLP + 40% LLM   (per category, 0-100)

5 categories scored: Discovery / Active Listening / Objection Handling /
Value Articulation / Closing & Next Steps. Each 0-20, summed to 0-100.

WHY BLEND: LLMs biased toward niceness (everyone gets 80). NLP biased toward
keyword stuffing. Together they're more honest than either alone.


------------------------ TOP 6 VIVA Q&A -------------------------------------

Q1. Why not just use ChatGPT to roleplay?
A.  Privacy (local Ollama, sales data never leaves), consistency (persona
    drift in raw GPT, ours has explicit personality dimensions), measurable
    feedback (we built the 3-tier evaluation; ChatGPT doesn't grade you).

Q2. Why fine-tune 6 small classifiers instead of one big LLM?
A.  Speed (CPU, <100ms total vs 3-5s LLM), cost (LLM only fires when we
    need generation), determinism (classifiers reproducible, LLMs vary),
    auditability (we can cite a classifier output, can't cite a hallucin.)

Q4. What's the LSTM doing that the classifiers can't?
A.  Classifiers see ONE turn. LSTM sees the SEQUENCE. A conversation can
    have good per-turn signals but a falling trajectory. Late-turn accuracy
    70.6% means it catches drift when it matters most.

Q6. Why hybrid evaluation? Why not just trust the LLM?
A.  LLMs grade everyone 75-85 (niceness bias). NLP rewards keyword stuffing.
    60/40 blend is more honest than either alone. Two assessors > one.

Q8. What if Ollama dies mid-conversation?
A.  Persona Agent returns error, frontend shows clean message, session
    preserved in DB. Classifiers + LSTM keep working (no Ollama dependency).
    Post-session evaluation can be re-run when Ollama is back.

Q11. What's the per-turn latency budget?
A.  Guardrail 5ms + EQ 50ms + Knowledge 200ms + small agents 10ms +
    classifiers 80ms + LSTM 5ms + PERSONA AGENT 3-5s (bottleneck) =
    end-to-end ~3.5-5.5s. SalesRL subprocess doesn't block.

==============================================================================
   THE EQ METRIC — WHAT IT REPRESENTS (MOST COMMON VIVA QUESTION)
==============================================================================

  EQ (0-100) = the TRAINEE'S emotional intelligence score this session.
  Updated per turn. Rolling history kept for trend tracking.

------------------------ THE FORMULA ---------------------------------------

  EQ = 0.30 * Empathy      (from Emotion-RoBERTa, 28-class GoEmotions)
     + 0.25 * Listening    (from DeBERTa NLI semantic similarity)
     + 0.25 * (1-Pressure) (from Emotion-RoBERTa pressure head)
     + 0.20 * LAER         (regex detection of Listen-Acknowledge-
                            Explore-Respond patterns)


------------------------ THE 4 SIGNALS IN PLAIN ENGLISH --------------------

  EMPATHY (30% weight)
    HIGH: "I completely understand your budget concerns"
    LOW:  "let me just pitch you harder then"

  ACTIVE LISTENING (25% weight, semantic similarity)
    HIGH: Customer says "HubSpot was too complex" -> trainee: "our tool
          is simpler than HubSpot because..." (addresses SPECIFICALLY)
    LOW:  Customer says "too complex" -> trainee: "what's your budget?"
          (ignores, pivots)

  PRESSURE (25% weight, INVERTED -> less pressure = higher EQ)
    HIGH pressure (bad): "you're losing money every day you wait"
    LOW pressure (good): "when would be a good time to revisit this?"

  LAER (20% weight, Carew International framework)
    L - Listen:      no interruption, full attention
    A - Acknowledge: "that's a fair concern"
    E - Explore:     "can you tell me more about that?"
    R - Respond:     evidence-based rebuttal
    Each detected step = +0.25. All 4 steps = LAER score 1.0.


------------------------ WLEIS MAPPING (ACADEMIC LINK) ---------------------

  Your EQ maps to the Wong & Law 2002 Emotional Intelligence Scale:

    WLEIS dimension                     Our mapping
    ---------------------------------   -------------------
    OEA (Others' Emotion Appraisal)     Empathy signal
    ROE (Regulation of Emotion)         1 - Pressure
    UOE (Use of Emotion)                Active Listening
    SEA (Self-Emotion Appraisal)        Composite EQ score

  Citation: Wong, C.S. & Law, K.S. (2002). Leadership Quarterly, 13(3).


------------------------ EQ TRIGGERS ADAPTIVE DIFFICULTY -------------------

  EQ is not just displayed — it FEEDS BACK into the persona's behaviour:

    Trainee low EQ (pushy, dismissive)
        |
        v
    Adaptive Difficulty Agent detects declining trend
        |
        v
    Injects "cooler/ice_cold" directive into Persona LLM prompt
        |
        v
    AI customer becomes MORE resistant the next turn

  And vice versa: high EQ -> customer softens, opens up.
  Creates a reinforcement loop that rewards empathetic selling.


------------------------ ONE-LINER FOR PANEL -------------------------------

  "EQ is Wong and Law 2002 WLEIS-based — four dimensions combined weighted:
   empathy from Emotion-RoBERTa, active listening from DeBERTa NLI semantic
   similarity, composure from our fine-tuned pressure classifier, and LAER
   detection via regex from the Carew International framework. Real-time
   per-turn scoring that feeds back into the Adaptive Difficulty Agent —
   if trainee is empathetic, the AI customer warms up; if pushy, it turns
   cold. Creates a loop that teaches emotional intelligence by doing."
