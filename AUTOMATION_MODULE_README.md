# SalesForge — Automation Module (AI Lead Engagement & Qualification)

## Overview
The **Automation Module** (often referred to internally as Module 5a) is a hybrid ML-LLM B2B Lead Engagement & Qualification Pipeline. It acts as an autonomous sales assistant that takes raw lead data, scores its quality using Machine Learning, and automatically reaches out to the most promising prospects with highly personalized, context-aware emails generated by an LLM.

The core goal of this module is to **scale personalized outbound sales outreach** without sacrificing quality, ensuring human sales representatives only spend their time on high-intent prospects or complex conversations.

---

## Key Features & Architecture

### 1. ML-Powered Lead Scoring & Allocation
When a new list of B2B leads is uploaded (via CSV), a pre-trained **XGBoost model** automatically evaluates them.
- It calculates a **Win Probability** (0 to 1) based on firmographic data (Industry, Employee Count, Revenue, Job Title).
- **Auto-Allocation Logic** *(counterintuitive — see rationale below)*:
  - **High Confidence ($\ge$ 60%) → MANUAL_REVIEW:** Human sales rep takes over. These are high-value deals where the cost of an AI mishandling an objection outweighs the time saved.
  - **Mid Confidence (10–60%) → AI_OUTREACH:** AI engages with a personalised, RAG-grounded cold email. This is the bulk of leads — where personalisation at scale is only possible with AI.
  - **Low Confidence (< 10%) → NURTURE_CAMPAIGN:** Cold leads enter a low-touch drip campaign for periodic re-engagement.

**Why high-confidence → human, not AI?** When the model is highly confident a lead will convert, the deal is high-stakes. A human can navigate complex multi-stakeholder buying decisions, custom pricing, and edge-case objections that an LLM-driven AI would mishandle. Letting AI loose on whale accounts is the kind of mistake that costs more than the time it saves. AI handles the long tail of mid-tier leads where the alternative isn't a human rep — it's *no engagement at all*.

### 2. Context-Aware AI Drafting (RAG Integration)
For leads allocated to the AI, the backend uses **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** to draft outbound emails.
- It queries the company's Vector Database (ChromaDB) for relevant sales playbooks, value propositions, and capabilities tailored to the prospect's industry.
- The local LLM (Ollama) uses this grounded knowledge to craft a highly personalized message that avoids generic "spray and pray" templates.

### 3. The 3-Stage Quality Control Pipeline
To ensure the AI never sends embarrassing mistakes, identity leaks, or unpolished text, the system securely passes every email through a strict 3-stage validation pipeline:

1. **The Draft Stage:** The LLM generates the initial email using a strict prompt enforcing sender identity, length limits, and banning template placeholders (e.g., `[Insert Name]`).
2. **Deterministic Validator (Regex):** A high-speed programmatic check that blindly strips any accidental bracket placeholders, enforces a mandatory sign-off from the real user/company, and trims excessive lengths.
3. **LLM Quality-Check Agent:** A second independent agent call that acts as a manager. It evaluates the clean draft against 5 strict criteria:
   - **Identity:** No brand name bleed from RAG documents.
   - **Placeholders:** No remaining unfilled template fields.
   - **Length:** Stays under 150 words.
   - **Personalization:** Properly addresses the prospect's company and role.
   - **CTA:** Ends with a singular, clear Call-to-Action.
   *(If the email fails this check, the Quality Agent rewrites it automatically).*

### 4. Real SMTP Transmission & Tracking
Once an email clears the Quality Control Pipeline, it is natively sent via `smtplib` using the authenticated user's actual email service provider (e.g., Gmail SMTP). 
- Interactions are logged into a JSON-based `conversation_state` in the `AutomatedOutreach` database table.
- The system gracefully transitions lead statuses (e.g., `PENDING` $\rightarrow$ `DRAFTING_OUTREACH` $\rightarrow$ `AI_ACTIVE`).

### 5. Asynchronous Background Processing
All of this heavy processing runs securely in the background using **FastAPI BackgroundTasks**. The frontend remains completely responsive while the backend systematically clears the queue, generating, validating, and dispatching dozens of unique emails.

## How it Fits into SalesForge
- **Frontend (`LeadManager.jsx`):** Allows users to map CSV uploads, define Campaign Goals ("Push for a 15 min discovery call"), and trigger bulk outreach. Status badges provide real-time visibility.
- **Backend (`services/outreach_service.py`):** The orchestrator of the entire process, bridging the database, LangChain/Chroma RAG systems, Ollama LLM API, and SMTP dispatcher.
- **Database (`models/lead.py`):** Robustly tracks outcomes, historical conversations, and ML allocation decisions.
